\subsubsubsection{$\mu(n)$ 的前缀和}

根据 $\mu * \mathbf{1} = \varepsilon$，容易得出以下结论：
\[
\sum_{i = 1}^{n} \mathbf{1}(i) = n,\qquad 
\sum_{i = 1}^{n} \varepsilon(i) = 1.
\]

\subsubsubsection{$\varphi(n)$ 的前缀和}

根据 $\varphi * \mathbf{1} = \operatorname{Id}$，容易得出以下结论：
\[
\sum_{i = 1}^{n} \mathbf{1}(i) = n,\qquad 
\sum_{i = 1}^{n} \operatorname{Id}(i) = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
\]
